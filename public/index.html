    Track record

    Every weekday the board is saved a few minutes before the US open and checked one hour after. Results are committed to GitHub as they happen.

      How to read this

      Coinbase tokenized stocks trade on Base around the clock, but the Chainlink price most apps use stops moving when the US market closes. The gap between that frozen price and what people pay on Base right now is the market's guess at Monday's open. Hyperliquid's stock perps trade 24/7 too, so when both venues agree, the move is probably real.

      Trading is available only to people outside the United States, in places where these tokens are legal. Swaps route through KyberSwap and carry an onchain attribution tag for this app. Nothing here is investment advice.
